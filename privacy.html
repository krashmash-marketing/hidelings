Privacy Policy

Hidelings for iPhone · Last updated October 19, 2026

Hidelings is made for children and their families. The short version: we do not collect any data.

What we collect

Nothing. Hidelings has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code (SDKs). The app does not send any information about you or your child to us or to anyone else.

Camera

The app uses the camera to show the room in augmented reality, so grown-ups can place characters and kids can find them. The camera image is processed on the iPhone in real time. It is not recorded and not uploaded.

What stays on your iPhone

When you save a quest, the app stores on your device: the quest settings (theme, age, riddles, your hints), a map of the room that lets the app find the characters again, and one photo of the room taken when you started placing characters, to help the app recognize the room later. These files never leave the device. Deleting a quest in the app, or deleting the app, removes them.

If your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) is turned on, these files may be included in that backup, which is handled by Apple under your own settings.

Purchases

Extra themes are bought through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app only learns which themes are unlocked. Purchases are protected by a parental gate.

Children

Hidelings is designed for children aged 3–11 and complies with the App Store Kids Category rules. Because we collect no personal information, we have no personal information about children to share, sell or delete. Purchases, links and settings are behind a grown-up check.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above. We will not start collecting data without updating the app and this policy first.
