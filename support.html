Hidelings for iPhone

Need help or found a bug? Write to krashmash@gmail.com. We usually reply within two days.

How to play

  1. Grown-up: pick a theme and your child’s age, then walk slowly around the room with the camera.
  2. Tap a table, shelf, floor or wall to place a character. Place 3–6 characters, then the treasure chest where the real gift is hidden.
  3. Tap Save and hand the iPhone to your child.
  4. Kid: follow the warmer/colder hints, find each character, tap it and solve its riddle. The last one leads to the chest.

Tips for a smooth hunt

  • Play in a well-lit room. Plain white walls and dark rooms are hard for the camera.
  • Move the iPhone slowly while placing characters.
  • Don’t move furniture between hiding and finding.
  • If a saved quest can’t find the room, stand where you started and point the camera as in the photo the app shows.

Purchases

Bought a theme on another device or reinstalled the app? Open For parents (top right on the home screen) and tap Restore purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Which iPhones work?

Any iPhone with iOS 18 or later. iPhone Pro models with LiDAR find hidden characters a bit more precisely, but every supported iPhone can play.
